Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At DreamKey Properties, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while using our website, enquiry forms, property services, and related communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, property requirement, preferred location, budget, enquiry details, and any message you submit through our website or contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, share suitable property options, arrange follow-ups, provide buying, selling, or rental guidance, improve our services, and maintain customer communication records.

3. Property Enquiry Data

When you submit a property enquiry through our website, your details may be stored securely and used by DreamKey Properties to contact you regarding verified homes, rental listings, property visits, or related real estate support.

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. However, no online method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services for hosting, email communication, analytics, maps, forms, or website performance. These services may process limited data as required to support website functionality.

6. Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve browsing experience, understand website usage, enhance performance, and provide a smoother user experience.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your details may only be shared when required to provide requested property services, coordinate with relevant service partners, comply with legal obligations, or support website operations.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us to request correction, update, or removal of your personal information from our records, where applicable and subject to legal or business record requirements.

9. Policy Updates

DreamKey Properties may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact DreamKey Properties.

Email: contact@dreamkeyproperties.com
